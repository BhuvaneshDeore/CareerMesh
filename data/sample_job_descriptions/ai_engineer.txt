Job Title: AI Engineer (Generative AI & LLMs)
Seniority: Mid-Level
Department: Applied AI Engineering

About the Role:
We are looking for an AI Engineer to build, evaluate, and deploy scalable Generative AI and LLM applications. You will collaborate with product and platform teams to design retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines and production-grade intelligent services.

Responsibilities:
- Design and implement RAG pipelines and autonomous agent workflows.
- Build production-ready REST APIs using FastAPI to serve model inferences.
- Collaborate with MLOps engineers to containerize workloads using Docker and deploy to AWS.
- Monitor model performance, latency, and context window efficiency in production.
- Write unit tests and maintain high code quality with Git and CI/CD pipelines.

Required Qualifications:
- 2+ years of software development experience with strong proficiency in Python and SQL.
- Deep hands-on experience with Machine Learning and Deep Learning frameworks (PyTorch or TensorFlow).
- Practical experience with Large Language Models (LLMs), LangChain, and Prompt Engineering.
- Familiarity with Vector Databases (such as Pinecone or ChromaDB) and RAG architecture.
- Hands-on experience with Docker containerization and Git version control.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with FastAPI or Flask for serving backend microservices.
- Experience with MLOps tools (MLflow or DVC) and cloud platforms (AWS or GCP).
- Understanding of transformer model architectures and Hugging Face pipelines.
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related engineering discipline.
