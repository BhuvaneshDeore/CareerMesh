Job Title: Data Scientist
Seniority: Junior / Mid-Level
Department: Data Analytics & Insights

About the Role:
We are seeking a curious, analytical Data Scientist to turn complex business datasets into predictive models and strategic insights.

Responsibilities:
- Perform exploratory data analysis and build predictive statistical models.
- Write robust, optimized SQL queries and manage ETL data pipelines.
- Build classification and regression models using Scikit-Learn and Pandas.
- Present data visualizations and experimental findings to cross-functional stakeholders.

Required Qualifications:
- 1+ years of experience analyzing structured and unstructured data using Python and SQL.
- Strong knowledge of Statistics, Data Analysis, and Data Structures & Algorithms.
- Hands-on experience with Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Scikit-Learn.
- Experience utilizing Git for collaborative version control.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Familiarity with Cloud platforms (GCP or AWS).
- Basic exposure to Deep Learning or PyTorch.
- Experience with Docker and containerized deployment.
